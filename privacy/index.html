Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Clean Date ("we", "us") collects when you use the Clean Date app and website at cleandate.app, including the Android and iPhone apps (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. Clean Date is for adults 18 and older.

The short version: we collect what you put in your profile so other members can find you. We use it only to run Clean Date. We never sell your personal information, and we don’t show ads inside the app.

1. Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (or your Google sign-in). Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see them.
  • Profile: name, age, gender, city/town, job, height, whether you have kids, smoking, religion, what you’re looking for, interests, bio, and photos.
  • Sobriety and recovery details (optional): things like how long you’ve been sober, your recovery program, or substances you choose to list. This is sensitive information. Only share what you’re comfortable with. By adding it, you consent to our storing it and showing it as described below. You can edit or remove it at any time, and you can turn off "Show my sobriety time to matches" in Settings.
  • Likes, matches, and messages: who you like (including Clean Likes), your matches, chat messages, and photos you send in chat. If you unmatch or report someone, we keep a record of that, including the reason you pick.
  • Forms: if you use Contact support, "Get updates" on our home page, or "Notify me" for Premium, we receive your email and what you write.

2. Location

Location is required so Clean Date can show you people nearby. With your permission, the app reads your device location and rounds it to about 1 km (2 decimal places) before saving it. To fill in your city, the app may send those rounded coordinates, or the city you type, to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service. Other members see your city and an approximate distance, never your exact location. You can type a city instead of sharing device location, and you can turn location off in your phone’s settings.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Visit counts: our website and the browser version of the app add one to anonymous counters (Abacus, abacus.jasoncameron.dev) the first time a browser visits each day. Those counters record the page, the date, and a broad source like "TikTok" or "Direct". They store no names, emails, IP-based profiles, or cookies. The installed Android/iPhone apps aren’t counted. To mark that a visit was already counted, your browser keeps a small note in its local storage.
  • On your device: the app saves some settings and recent data in your browser’s or app’s local storage so it loads quickly.
  • Technical data: our hosting (GitHub Pages) and service providers may log standard technical information like IP address and browser type for security and operation.

4. How we use information

  • to create your account and show your profile to other members;
  • to suggest people near you that fit your filters, and to deliver likes, matches, messages, and calls;
  • to keep members safe: reviewing reports, blocking, and enforcing our Terms;
  • to answer support requests and send updates you asked for;
  • to understand roughly how many people visit and where they come from, so we can improve Clean Date.

5. Who can see your information

  • Other members: signed-in members can see your profile (photos, name, age, city, bio, interests, and other details you add) and an approximate distance. Messages and call details are visible only to you and your match. If you hide it, your sobriety time isn’t shown to matches.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store accounts, profiles, photos, likes, matches, and messages. We use Web3Forms to deliver support and update forms to our inbox, OpenStreetMap Nominatim for city lookups, Abacus for anonymous visit counts, and GitHub Pages for hosting. Voice and video calls connect directly between devices using WebRTC, with help from public connection servers (such as Google’s STUN server) and, when a direct connection isn’t possible, a relay service. These providers handle data only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal and safety: we may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety or the rights of Clean Date and its members.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is active. To delete your account, go to Settings → Delete account, or send a deletion request through the Contact support form in the app. We’ll remove your profile, photos, likes, matches, and messages within a few days. We may keep limited records, such as safety reports, for as long as needed to protect members or comply with the law. Messages you sent may stay visible to the person you sent them to until their copy is removed. To stop appearing in Discover without deleting, use Pause my profile.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile, remove optional details, change your photos, pause your profile, and unmatch or report anyone at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, through the in-app Contact support form.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the Connecticut Data Privacy Act), you may have additional rights. We will honor them as the law requires.

8. Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and protected by access rules that limit who can read it. No system is perfectly secure, though, so please don’t share anything you would be very uncomfortable having exposed.

9. Children

Clean Date is only for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will delete the account.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Clean Date grows. If we make important changes, we’ll update the effective date and let you know in the app or on the website.

11. Contact

Questions or requests? Reach us through the Contact support form in the app (Profile → Settings → Contact support). Clean Date is operated in Connecticut, USA.
